# Demo Video Script — Lead Pipeline Automation (60 seconds)

**Setup:** open the public demo page (`/`), start screen recording. Click
“Fill: 🔥 Hot lead” so the form is ready. Speak naturally — timings are
approximate.

---

**[0:00–0:08] Hook**

> "Most small businesses lose leads the same way: someone fills out the website
> form at 9 PM… and nobody replies until Tuesday. By then they've called your
> competitor. This pipeline fixes that — automatically."

*Action: hero + the two-column demo layout visible.*

**[0:08–0:20] Submit the lead**

> "I'm the website visitor. I submit a quote request — and watch the pipeline
> catch it."

*Action: click “Submit test lead”. Point at stage 1 lighting up, then the run
log lines appearing.*

**[0:20–0:35] Scoring + CRM**

> "First it scores the lead — 95 out of 100, hot — and shows exactly why:
> budget, urgency keywords, business email. No black box. Then it saves
> everything to the CRM with a full run history."

*Action: stages 2 and 3 complete; scroll to the result card, point at the
score bar and the “Why this score” list.*

**[0:35–0:48] Follow-ups + Slack alert**

> "Then it schedules the follow-ups — instant SMS and email today, a call task
> for the owner tomorrow. And because this lead is hot, the owner gets a Slack
> alert within seconds — while the lead is still thinking about you."

*Action: point at the follow-up timeline, then the mock #new-leads Slack card.*

**[0:48–0:55] Admin view**

> "And it all lands here — every lead, scored and filterable, with its full
> history."

*Action: switch to `/admin`, toggle the Hot/Warm/Cold filters, expand one
lead.*

**[0:55–1:00] Close**

> "This is a concept demo — but the real version is a genuine n8n workflow,
> ready to import, wired to your real CRM, email and Slack. If missed inquiries
> are costing you jobs, let's talk."

---

**Recording tips**

- Do one full off-camera run first, then “Clear demo data” on `/admin` right
  before recording for a clean take.
- The pipeline run takes ~6 seconds — don't rush the narration; let each stage
  land.
- For contrast, record a second 15-second clip with the “🔵 Cold / spam”
  preset to show the spam flag and the skipped Slack alert.
- Keep the browser at ~1280px wide so the form and pipeline sit side by side.
